SparseRouter: Mixture-of-Prompts Retrieval for Instruction-Tuned LLMs
Abstract
SparseRouter routes each instruction to one of eight prompt experts and improves exact match on
NaturalQA by 4.1 points over a dense retriever.
1 Introduction
Instruction-tuned language models depend on the retrieved context. We propose a sparse router
over prompt experts and evaluate it with greedy decoding.
2 Method
The router scores experts with a learned linear layer and keeps the top 2 experts per query.
3 Experiments
3.1 Setup
We decode with temperature 0.2, top_p = 0.9 and a maximum of 256 new tokens. All runs use
random seed 13. We fine-tune with the AdamW optimizer, learning rate 2e-5 and batch size 32 for
3 epochs.

4 Limitations
Performance degrades on prompts longer than 4096 tokens, and the router is sensitive to noisy
instructions.
