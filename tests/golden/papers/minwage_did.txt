Minimum Wages and Teen Employment: Evidence from County Borders
Abstract
Using a difference-in-differences design across contiguous county pairs, we find that a 10%
minimum wage increase reduces teen employment by 1.2%.
1 Introduction
We compare contiguous counties on either side of state borders.
2 Empirical Strategy
We estimate a two-way fixed effects model with county-pair by period effects. Identification
assumes parallel trends between paired counties, and standard errors are clustered by state.
