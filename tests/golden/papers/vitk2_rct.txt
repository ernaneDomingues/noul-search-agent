Effect of Daily Vitamin K2 on Arterial Stiffness: A Randomized Controlled Trial
Abstract
In a double-blind randomized controlled trial of 240 adults, daily vitamin K2 for 12 months
reduced pulse-wave velocity by 0.4 m/s compared with placebo.
1 Introduction
Arterial stiffness predicts cardiovascular events. Vitamin K2 activates matrix Gla protein.
2 Methods
2.1 Participants
We randomized 240 adults aged 50 to 75 years to vitamin K2 (180 ug/day) or placebo. A power
calculation assumed a difference of 0.4 m/s with 80% power.
2.2 Outcomes
The primary outcome was carotid-femoral pulse-wave velocity at 12 months.

4 Discussion
The effect was smaller in participants with diabetes, which suggests a population limitation.
5 Limitations
The trial included mostly white participants from one city, so results may not generalize.
