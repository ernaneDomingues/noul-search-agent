Graph Kernels for Molecule Ranking
Abstract
We present a graph kernel that ranks candidate molecules and reaches 0.91 AUC on the screening
benchmark.
1 Introduction
Ranking candidate molecules is expensive. We propose a kernel over labelled molecular graphs.
2 Method
The kernel counts shared rooted subtrees up to depth three.
3 Experiments
Table 1: AUC on the screening benchmark.
Method AUC
Fingerprint baseline 0.86
Graph kernel (ours) 0.91
The kernel improves AUC by 0.05 over fingerprints.
