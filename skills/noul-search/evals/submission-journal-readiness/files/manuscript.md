# Vitamin K2 and arterial stiffness: a randomised controlled trial

## Abstract
In 240 adults, daily vitamin K2 for 12 months reduced pulse-wave velocity by 0.4 m/s compared with placebo.

## Introduction
Arterial stiffness predicts cardiovascular events.

## Methods
Adults aged 50-75 were randomised 1:1 to vitamin K2 or placebo. The primary outcome was carotid-femoral pulse-wave velocity.

## Results
Pulse-wave velocity decreased by 0.3 m/s with vitamin K2 and increased by 0.1 m/s with placebo.

## Discussion
Vitamin K2 may slow arterial stiffening.
