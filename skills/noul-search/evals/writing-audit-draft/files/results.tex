\section{Results}
Our method clearly proves that sparse routing is superior, achieving state-of-the-art performance across all tasks.
We minimize $L_{total}$ during pre-training and then fine-tune with $\mathcal{L}_{total}$ on each benchmark.
Table~\ref{tab:main} shows an accuracy of 91.2\% on GLUE, compared with 88.0\% for the dense baseline.
The improvement is obviously significant and will generalize to any domain.
